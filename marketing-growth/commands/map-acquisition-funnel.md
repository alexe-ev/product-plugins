---
description: Map the acquisition funnel from awareness to activation and identify conversion gaps. Use this skill when a team needs to understand where users are dropping out before becoming active.
---

Ask the user for: product and acquisition channels, available funnel data (even rough estimates by stage). Optionally: channel breakdown of funnel performance, segment breakdown, historical funnel trends, competitor funnel benchmarks.

Apply the skill at skills/map-acquisition-funnel/SKILL.md and REFERENCE.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: funnel stage definitions, conversion rates per stage, absolute drop-off volumes per stage, channel breakdown (if available), biggest conversion gaps prioritized by impact, benchmark comparison, priority improvement areas with rationale.
