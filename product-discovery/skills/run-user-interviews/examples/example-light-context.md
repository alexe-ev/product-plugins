## Input

Product: Finly — a B2C mobile app for personal finance.

Context: "We want to interview users about why they churn. We haven't decided how many interviews or exactly what we want to learn."

## Expected output

Research goal (provisional): Understand why users stop using Finly after initial sign-up. The specific decision this research should inform has not yet been defined — this should be confirmed before the guide is finalised.

Participant profile (suggested): Users who signed up in the last 90 days and have not returned after their first week. Target 6–8 interviews for pattern saturation. Consider adding 3–4 retained users for contrast if resources allow.

Interview guide (starter draft — review before use):

Warm-up:
- "How do you manage your finances day-to-day?"
- "What made you look for a personal finance app?"

Context-setting:
- "Walk me through what happened when you first signed up. What were you hoping the app would do?"
- "What did you actually use it for in the first few days?"

Core questions:
- "Was there a moment when you thought you might not keep using the app? Tell me about that."
- "What would have made you more likely to come back?"
- "What do you use instead when you want to think about your money?"

Wrap-up:
- "What would make an app like this worth using regularly for you?"

Note: This guide is a starting point. Before running interviews, confirm:
- The specific decision these interviews will inform
- Whether retained users should be included for contrast
- Note-taking and synthesis protocol

Number of interviews: Recommend 6–8 churned users as a minimum. Add 3–4 retained users if possible — the contrast often produces the most useful findings. Finalise count once research goal is confirmed.

Missing before finalising: Specific decision this research must inform; whether to include retained users; note-taking assignment; synthesis timeline and format.
