---
description: Turn a product problem, idea, observation, or business goal into a clear testable experiment hypothesis. Use this skill when the user has an idea or problem but not yet a properly structured hypothesis for an A/B test or product experiment.
---

Ask the user for: product context or rough idea, problem statement, opportunity, observation, or desired outcome. Optionally: target segment, current metrics, business goal, funnel context, prior research, qualitative insights, constraints.

Apply the skill at skills/formulate-experiment-hypothesis/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: hypothesis statement, target segment, likely primary metric, expected direction of impact, why this might work, expected impact hypothesis (if evidence-based), missing information, confidence level of the hypothesis framing.
