description: Evaluate AI model capabilities, limitations, and trade-offs relevant to a product decision. Use this skill when a team needs to understand what an AI model can and cannot reliably do for their use case.

Ask the user for: product use case description, expected model behavior. Optionally: model options being considered, technical constraints (latency, cost, privacy), quality bar required, edge cases that must be handled.

Apply the skill at skills/assess-model-capabilities/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: task definition, model fit assessment, known failure modes and edge cases, trade-off comparison, recommended approach with rationale, required validation before production deployment, risks.
