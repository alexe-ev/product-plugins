---
description: Define the scope, goals, and methods for a product discovery research initiative. Use this skill before running any discovery research to ensure the team is investigating the right questions with the right methods.
---

Ask the user for: product area or problem space being explored, business or product decisions the research should inform. Optionally: existing assumptions or hypotheses, available time and resources, prior research on the same topic, team constraints.

Apply the skill at skills/scope-discovery-research/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: research goal and decisions it will inform, key research questions (prioritized), known vs. unknown assumptions, methods per question with rationale, participant criteria and volume, timeline and output format, risk and contingency plan.
