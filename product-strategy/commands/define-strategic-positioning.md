---
description: Define a clear strategic positioning statement that articulates where the product plays, who it serves best, and what makes it the right choice for that segment. Use this skill when the team needs to anchor strategy in a specific market position.
---

Ask the user for: target segment (or candidate segments), product capabilities and strengths, business goals. Optionally: product-market fit assessment, competitive landscape, win/loss data, ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) definition.

Apply the skill at skills/define-strategic-positioning/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: target segment (with explicit exclusions), frame of reference, point of differentiation, reason to believe, positioning statement, positioning trade-offs (what this positioning sacrifices), confidence level (provisional / context-informed / validated).
