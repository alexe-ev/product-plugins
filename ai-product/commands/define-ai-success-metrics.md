Ask the user for: AI feature description and target user, business goal the AI feature serves. Optionally: model capability assessment, human-in-the-loop design, prior user research on the problem area, regulatory context.

Apply the skill at skills/define-ai-success-metrics/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: model quality metrics, user experience metrics, business impact metrics, safety and risk metrics, baseline and target values, primary metric, review cadence and metric owners.
