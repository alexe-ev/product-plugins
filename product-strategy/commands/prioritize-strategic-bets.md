description: Evaluate and prioritize strategic bets — high-uncertainty, high-potential initiatives — against business goals and risk tolerance. Use this skill when a team needs to decide which big bets to pursue, pause, or stop.
---

Ask the user for: list of strategic bets or initiatives under consideration, business goals or strategic objectives, rough resource constraints. Optionally: PMF assessment per bet, competitive context, risk tolerance framing from leadership, time horizon.

Apply the skill at skills/prioritize-strategic-bets/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: bet inventory with (goal, hypothesis, investment, payoff potential), assessment matrix (alignment / value / confidence / reversibility), portfolio classification per bet, prioritization recommendation with rationale, what is being deprioritized and why, review triggers and milestones.
