---
description: Design product packaging tiers that align value delivery with willingness to pay and strategic segment goals. Use this skill when a product needs tiered packaging (free/starter/pro/enterprise) or when existing tiers need restructuring.
---

Ask the user for: product capabilities available to tier, target customer segments (at least 2-3 distinct profiles), business model (freemium, self-serve SaaS, enterprise, etc.). Optionally: current pricing and packaging, customer feedback on tiers, expansion revenue patterns, competitive tier structures.

Apply the skill at skills/design-packaging-tiers/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: value metric, tier structure with target segment and included capabilities, upgrade triggers per tier, features in free vs. paid tiers with rationale, conversion path design, tier cannibalization risks, validation steps before launch.
