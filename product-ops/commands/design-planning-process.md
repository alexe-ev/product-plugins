---
description: Design a product planning process that connects strategy to execution through a repeatable cycle of goal setting, roadmapping, and delivery. Use this skill when a team's planning is ad-hoc, inconsistent, or disconnected from strategy.
---

Ask the user for: team size and structure, planning horizon (annual, quarterly, 6-week cycles). Optionally: current planning process and pain points, company-level planning calendar, OKR or goal-setting framework in use, cross-team dependencies.

Apply the skill at skills/design-planning-process/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: planning levels and their purposes, each level (inputs, outputs, participants, timeline, decision rights), planning cascade design, key planning events calendar, strategy change protocol, documentation artifacts per level.
