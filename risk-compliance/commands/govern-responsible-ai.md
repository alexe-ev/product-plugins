description: Establish governance practices for responsible AI development including fairness, transparency, and accountability. Use this skill when a team building AI features needs a governance framework for responsible development.

Ask the user for: AI feature or product being governed, organizational context (team size, AI maturity, industry). Optionally: existing AI ethics principles, regulatory context, known AI risks in the product area, stakeholder expectations.

Apply the skill at skills/govern-responsible-ai/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: AI governance principles, requirements per principle, accountability structure, transparency mechanisms, fairness criteria and assessment approach, audit and review process, escalation paths, governance maturity level.
