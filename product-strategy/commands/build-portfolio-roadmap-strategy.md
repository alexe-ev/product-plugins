---
description: Structure product portfolio decisions and translate strategy into a prioritized roadmap. Use this skill when a team needs to align investment decisions with strategic goals and build a coherent roadmap.
---

Ask the user for: current product areas or bets (even rough list), business goals or strategic priorities, any constraints (team size, budget, timeline). Optionally: existing roadmap or backlog, customer segment priorities, product-market fit assessment per area, competitive context, OKRs or KPIs.

Apply the skill at skills/build-portfolio-roadmap-strategy/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: portfolio map (areas and their strategic classification), prioritization rationale, high-level roadmap structure (now / next / later), trade-offs and what is being deprioritized and why, key alignment decisions needed, confidence level (provisional / context-informed / ready-to-present).
