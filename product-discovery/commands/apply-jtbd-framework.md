---
description: Apply the Jobs-to-be-Done framework to understand what job users are hiring a product to do and why. Use this skill when a team needs to understand user motivation at a level deeper than features or demographics.
---

Ask the user for: product or feature area, available user research or interview data (even rough). Optionally: win/loss data, customer interviews about switching moments, churn reasons, competitive alternatives users consider.

Apply the skill at skills/apply-jtbd-framework/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: job statement(s), functional/emotional/social dimensions, struggling moment description, competing solutions for the same job, implications for product design and messaging, evidence quality, open questions.
