---
description: Plan, structure, and synthesize user interviews to generate reliable qualitative insights. Use this skill when a team needs to understand user behaviors, motivations, and problems through direct conversation.
---

Ask the user for: research goal or question to explore, target participant profile. Optionally: existing hypotheses to explore (not test), number of interviews planned, recruiting constraints, prior research context.

Apply the skill at skills/run-user-interviews/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: research goal and decisions it will inform, participant profile and recruiting criteria, interview guide (warm-up/context/core questions/wrap-up), note-taking protocol, synthesis plan, number of interviews needed with rationale.
