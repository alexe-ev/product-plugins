---
description: Evaluate how well a product or feature addresses real market needs and identify gaps or risks in product-market fit. Use this skill when a team needs to understand whether their product resonates with the target market.
---

Ask the user for: product description or area, target segment, available signals (retention data, NPS, qualitative feedback, growth metrics -- even rough). Optionally: churn rates or reasons, user interview summaries, comparison to competitors, business model context.

Apply the skill at skills/assess-product-market-fit/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: fit assessment level (pre-fit / early signals / strong signals / confirmed fit), key signals supporting the assessment, gaps and risks, recommended next steps, confidence level in the assessment.
