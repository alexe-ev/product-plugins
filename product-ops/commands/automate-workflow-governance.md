Ask the user for: current product workflows (even rough description), areas of manual overhead or bottleneck. Optionally: tools available for automation, team capacity and skills, governance requirements (compliance, security, etc.).

Apply the skill at skills/automate-workflow-governance/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: workflow inventory with time investment estimates, automation candidates with feasibility and value assessment, priority list of automation opportunities, governance standards for product operations, ownership and accountability map, risk assessment for workflow automation.
