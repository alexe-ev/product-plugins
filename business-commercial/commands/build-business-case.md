---
description: Develop a structured business case for a product investment including ROI, costs, risks, and expected outcomes. Use this skill when a team needs to justify a product investment to leadership or stakeholders.
---

Ask the user for: investment description and scope, business goal the investment serves, rough cost estimate (team time, tools, etc.). Optionally: revenue or growth impact estimate, current baseline metrics, alternative options considered, risk factors, strategic context.

Apply the skill at skills/build-business-case/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: investment summary, expected outcomes (quantitative and qualitative), financial impact estimate, cost breakdown, ROI or payback period with assumptions, risk assessment and mitigation, alternatives considered, recommendation with rationale.
