description: Identify and frame the real product problem or opportunity before jumping to solutions. Use this skill when a team has a rough signal, observation, or area of concern but hasn't yet defined what the actual problem is.
---

Ask the user for: raw signal (complaint, data observation, business goal, or team concern). Optionally: affected user segment, related metrics or data, customer feedback snippets, prior attempts to address the issue.

Apply the skill at skills/identify-problem-opportunity/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: problem statement (user-centric), affected segment, raw signal that triggered discovery, business impact, evidence quality, open questions to resolve, confidence level.
