Ask the user for: product flow or feature to assess, available signals (analytics data, user feedback, support tickets, or usability observations). Optionally: baseline metrics, prior usability test findings, design heuristics to apply.

Apply the skill at skills/assess-experience-quality/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: flow map with quality assessment per step, friction points by severity, heuristic violations, quick wins and structural improvements, metrics impact estimation, confidence level in the assessment.
