Ask the user for: business model type (subscription, transactional, usage-based, etc.), available unit economics data (even partial). Optionally: CAC by channel, churn rate, gross margin, expansion revenue data, cohort LTV data.

Apply the skill at skills/analyze-unit-economics/SKILL.md and REFERENCE.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: unit economics summary (CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC, payback period, gross margin), benchmark comparison, health assessment per metric, key risk or gap identification, improvement levers, assumptions and confidence level for each metric.
