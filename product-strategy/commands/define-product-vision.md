---
description: Turn organizational goals and user insights into a clear product vision and mission statement. Use this skill when a team needs to articulate where the product is going and why it exists.
---

Ask the user for: product area or domain, target user or customer segment, business goal or organizational context. Optionally: existing mission/vision drafts, key user problems or insights, company-level strategy, competitive landscape notes, time horizon for the vision.

Apply the skill at skills/define-product-vision/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: vision statement, mission statement, target segment, core user problem addressed, strategic time horizon, confidence level (provisional / context-informed / ready-to-align), missing context or open questions.
