---
description: Map key stakeholders, their interests and influence, and develop strategies to align and engage them. Use this skill when a team needs to navigate stakeholder dynamics for a product initiative.
---

Ask the user for: product initiative or decision requiring stakeholder engagement, list of stakeholders involved (even rough). Optionally: stakeholder interests and concerns, power/influence relationships, history of stakeholder dynamics, desired outcome from stakeholder engagement.

Apply the skill at skills/map-stakeholder-influence/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: stakeholder inventory with role/interest/influence/stance, influence-interest map, engagement strategy by quadrant, key blockers and mitigations, key allies and how to leverage them, engagement plan for high-priority stakeholders.
