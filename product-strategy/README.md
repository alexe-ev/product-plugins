# Product Strategy

Skills for setting long-term product direction, assessing market fit, making portfolio decisions, and shaping competitive positioning.

---

## Skills in this domain

**Vision & direction**
1. `define-product-vision` — Turn organizational goals into a clear product vision
2. `craft-product-mission` — Write a mission statement specific enough to guide trade-offs
3. `define-long-term-direction` — Translate vision and mission into a 2–4 year strategic trajectory

**Market fit & positioning**
4. `assess-product-market-fit` — Evaluate fit signals and identify gaps
5. `define-strategic-positioning` — Define where the product plays and who it's for

**Portfolio & roadmap strategy**
6. `build-portfolio-roadmap-strategy` — Translate strategy into a prioritized portfolio view
7. `prioritize-strategic-bets` — Evaluate and sequence high-uncertainty, high-potential bets

**Competitive strategy**
8. `analyze-competitive-landscape` — Map competitors and identify strategic implications
9. `frame-competitive-differentiation` — Define a defensible differentiation position
