---
description: Design a structured UX or product research study including method selection, protocol, stimuli, and success criteria. Use this skill when a team needs to go beyond ad-hoc interviews to a rigorous, repeatable research design.
---

Ask the user for: research question or goal, participant profile. Optionally: prototype or designs to evaluate, prior research findings, constraints (time, budget, participant access), required output format.

Apply the skill at skills/design-research-study/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: study goal and research questions, method selection with rationale, study protocol, participant profile and screening criteria, data capture plan, analysis approach and success criteria, pilot plan, known risks and mitigations.
