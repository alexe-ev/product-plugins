---
description: Build and validate user personas and segment definitions grounded in real research data. Use this skill when a team needs to define who they are building for with enough specificity to guide product decisions.
---

Ask the user for: available research data or user observations, product area or context. Optionally: existing personas or segment definitions, behavioral data (usage patterns, feature adoption), demographic or firmographic data (for B2B).

Apply the skill at skills/develop-persona-segment/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: per persona/segment: name and archetype, primary goals and jobs to be done, key pain points, context of use, behavioral patterns, strategic priority, evidence quality, research gaps.
