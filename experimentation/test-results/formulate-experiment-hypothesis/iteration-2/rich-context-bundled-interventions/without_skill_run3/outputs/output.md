# Experiment Hypothesis: Bundled Interventions for Conversion Optimization

## Context
- **Current Conversion Rate**: 3.2% (mobile web)
- **Target Conversion Rate**: 5.0%
- **Primary Segment**: Android smartphone buyers
- **Marketplace**: Used electronics marketplace
- **Planned Interventions**:
  1. Add seller video reviews to product cards
  2. Reduce checkout from 4 steps to 2 steps

## Hypothesis Statement

**If** we simultaneously implement seller video reviews on product cards and streamline the checkout process from 4 steps to 2 steps, **then** Android mobile web users purchasing smartphones will experience increased purchase intent and reduced friction in the conversion funnel, **resulting in** conversion rate improvement from 3.2% to 5.0% (56% lift).

## Rationale

### Video Reviews Intervention
- **Trust Building**: Video reviews from sellers add social proof and authenticity to used electronics listings, addressing buyer concerns about product condition and legitimacy in the second-hand market
- **Mobile-Optimized Content**: Video content performs well on mobile devices and captures attention more effectively than static images or text descriptions
- **Reduced Information Gap**: Video eliminates uncertainty about product functionality and cosmetic condition—critical factors for used smartphone purchases
- **Target Alignment**: Android buyers are typically mobile-native users who engage naturally with video content

### Checkout Simplification Intervention
- **Friction Reduction**: Reducing checkout steps from 4 to 2 eliminates decision fatigue and reduces cart abandonment
- **Mobile UX Enhancement**: Multi-step forms are particularly problematic on mobile; condensed checkout significantly improves completion rates
- **Decision Velocity**: Lower friction allows buyers with lower confidence (characteristic of second-hand purchases) to commit faster

### Combined Effect
The bundled approach addresses both **confidence barriers** (video reviews build trust) and **process barriers** (simplified checkout removes friction). This two-pronged intervention targets the complete conversion journey:
- **Consideration Phase**: Video reviews help buyers evaluate the product
- **Purchase Phase**: Simplified checkout enables quick transaction completion

## Success Criteria
- **Primary Metric**: Conversion rate increase from 3.2% to 5.0% (minimum 4.5% for statistical significance)
- **Secondary Metrics**:
  - Average time spent on product card (should increase due to video engagement)
  - Checkout completion rate (should improve with 2-step process)
  - Cart abandonment rate (should decrease)
  - Video play rate on product cards (measure content effectiveness)

## Experiment Design
- **Control Group**: Current state (no video, 4-step checkout)
- **Test Group**: Seller video + 2-step checkout
- **Duration**: Minimum 2-3 weeks to capture sufficient traffic and account for day-of-week variations
- **Sample Size**: Sufficient to detect 56% lift with 95% confidence and 80% power
- **Segmentation**: Focus on Android mobile web users; monitor if effect varies by device type or brand

## Risk Considerations
- **Video Production Burden**: Sellers may not universally provide high-quality videos; consider incentives or templates
- **Checkout UX Risk**: Aggressive consolidation from 4 to 2 steps could hide critical information; validate that essential fields aren't omitted
- **Confounding Variables**: Monitor external factors (market conditions, competitive activity) during the experiment period
