---
description: Map and analyze the competitive landscape to understand the competitive environment, key players, and strategic implications. Use this skill when a team needs a structured view of who they compete with and how the landscape is evolving.
---

Ask the user for: product area and target segment, known competitors (at least partially). Optionally: recent competitive moves or news, win/loss data, customer feedback on alternatives, industry or market reports.

Apply the skill at skills/analyze-competitive-landscape/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: competitor inventory (direct, indirect, status quo), competitor profiles (segment, value prop, capabilities, strategy), competitive dimension map, areas of commoditization vs. differentiation opportunity, competitive threats (short and long term), strategic opportunities surfaced by the analysis, confidence level and data currency.
