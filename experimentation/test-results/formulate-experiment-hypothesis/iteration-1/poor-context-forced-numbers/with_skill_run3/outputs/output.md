**Translation:** Conversion is falling, we need to do something. We're thinking of changing the button color. This should increase conversion by 30%.

**Recommendation:** Remove or flag the 30% assumption until:

## Recommendation for Next Steps

1. **Validate the problem:** Is conversion actually declining? How do you know? What does the trend data show?
2. **Root cause analysis:** Why is conversion declining? Is it likely a visual/UX issue, or something else (messaging, targeting, product)?
3. **Hypothesis refinement:** If you suspect button color, what specific color and why? What evidence supports this?
4. **Remove forced numbers:** Replace the 30% assumption with directional language until you have data or expert justification
5. **Define the metric:** Be explicit about what "conversion" means (CTR, submission rate, purchase, etc.)
6. **Segment clarity:** Who exactly are you optimizing for?

Once these are clarified, this hypothesis can be reframed as **context-informed** or **execution-ready candidate**.
