# Experiment Hypothesis: Seller Video Reviews + Streamlined Checkout for Android Users

## Context
- **Platform:** Used electronics marketplace
- **Current Baseline Conversion Rate:** 3.2% (mobile web)
- **Target Segment:** Android smartphone buyers
- **Target Conversion Rate:** 5.0%
- **Planned Interventions:**
  1. Add seller video reviews to product cards
  2. Reduce checkout from 4 steps to 2 steps

---

## Primary Hypothesis

**If we introduce seller video reviews on product cards AND streamline the checkout process from 4 steps to 2 steps for Android mobile web users, then conversion rate will increase from 3.2% to 5.0% or higher because:**

1. **Video reviews reduce purchase friction** – Video content from sellers provides authentic, dynamic product information that builds trust more effectively than static images and text. For used electronics, visual proof of condition, functionality, and specifications addresses the primary concern of used goods buyers: "What is the actual state of this item?"

2. **Reduced cognitive load on decision-making** – Seller videos allow potential buyers to make faster purchasing decisions by presenting multiple product attributes (condition, packaging, accessories, functionality demos) in a single, engaging format rather than requiring them to scroll through text descriptions and gallery images.

3. **Streamlined checkout removes abandonment barriers** – Reducing checkout steps from 4 to 2 decreases friction at the final purchase stage, particularly for mobile users who experience higher abandonment rates due to smaller screens and touch input fatigue. Each additional step increases the likelihood of drop-off.

4. **Mobile-first optimization for Android users** – Android smartphone buyers represent a price-sensitive segment that values efficiency. Both interventions (quick video verification + fast checkout) align with mobile user behavior preferences for speed and simplicity.

5. **Combined effect exceeds individual improvements** – The two changes work synergistically: video reviews increase purchase intent earlier in the funnel (product card engagement), while streamlined checkout captures that intent before it dissipates during the transaction process.

---

## Secondary Hypotheses (Supporting Claims)

### Hypothesis 2: Video Reviews as Trust Catalyst
Adding seller video reviews will increase click-through rate (CTR) from product cards by 15-25% for Android users because video content is more engaging than static content and specifically addresses the skepticism inherent in used goods purchases.

### Hypothesis 3: Checkout Friction Impact
Reducing checkout steps from 4 to 2 will decrease cart abandonment rate by 20-30% among Android mobile users because each step creates a decision point where users can exit the flow.

### Hypothesis 4: Segment-Specific Response
Android smartphone buyers will show higher relative improvement (proportional to baseline) than other device/product combinations because this segment prioritizes efficiency and affordability—both satisfied by the interventions.

---

## Key Assumptions

1. **Video quality:** Seller videos are of sufficient quality (clear, well-lit, authentic) to build trust rather than create doubt
2. **Checkout simplification:** The 4→2 step reduction maintains all critical safety/compliance elements (address verification, payment security, etc.)
3. **Video performance:** The additional video content can load within acceptable timeframes on diverse Android devices and network conditions
4. **User behavior:** Android smartphone buyers are representative of target audience and respond positively to both video content and streamlined flows
5. **Sample size:** The experiment will have sufficient duration and traffic to reach statistical significance (typically 2-4 weeks for e-commerce conversion tests)

---

## Expected Outcomes

- **Primary KPI:** Mobile web conversion rate increases from 3.2% to ≥5.0% (56% relative improvement)
- **Secondary KPIs:**
  - Product card engagement rate (video plays, CTR)
  - Cart abandonment rate
  - Average order value
  - Device-specific performance (Android vs. iOS comparison)
  - Video watch-through rate and completion percentage

---

## Experiment Design Considerations

- **Test population:** Android mobile web users only (can expand to iOS in follow-up test)
- **Control group:** Current experience (no videos, 4-step checkout)
- **Treatment group:** Videos enabled + 2-step checkout
- **Duration:** Run for minimum 2-3 weeks to account for weekly traffic variation
- **Segmentation:** Monitor results separately for smartphone category vs. other electronics categories
- **Statistical significance:** Target 95% confidence level with adequate sample size

---

## Rationale for Target Conversion (3.2% → 5.0%)

The 1.8 percentage point increase (56% relative lift) is ambitious but defensible:
- Video reviews typically increase conversion by 20-40% in e-commerce contexts
- Checkout optimization typically yields 15-30% conversion improvements
- Combined effects suggest 50-70% potential improvement
- 5.0% represents a conservative estimate (58% uplift) within this range and leaves headroom for real-world implementation challenges

---

## Risk Considerations

1. **Video content quality variance** – Poor seller videos could undermine trust instead of building it; mitigation: implement basic quality guidelines for videos
2. **Checkout usability issues** – Aggressive step reduction could create UX confusion; mitigation: user test the 2-step flow before launch
3. **Implementation complexity** – Video hosting, encoding, and mobile performance optimization could impact speed metrics; mitigation: plan infrastructure requirements upfront
4. **Segment-specific results** – The improvement may not hold uniformly across all used electronics categories; mitigation: segment analysis during test
