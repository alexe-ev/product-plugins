description: Analyze the competitive landscape and define a clear positioning and differentiation angle for the product. Use this skill when a team needs to understand how they stand out and why customers should choose them.
---

Ask the user for: product description and key capabilities, target segment, known competitors (at least 2-3). Optionally: customer feedback on why they chose your product, win/loss analysis, product-market fit assessment, pricing context, positioning statement drafts.

Apply the skill at skills/frame-competitive-differentiation/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: competitive landscape summary (direct and indirect alternatives), differentiation map (where you win / lose / are parity), primary differentiation angle, differentiation statement, defensibility assessment (how hard is this to copy?), risks and gaps, confidence level (provisional / context-informed / ready-to-use).
