Ask the user for: AI feature or system to audit, responsible AI standards to audit against. Optionally: AI governance framework, quality monitoring data, user feedback or complaints, prior audits or assessments.

Apply the skill at skills/conduct-ai-audit/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: audit scope and standards applied, evidence gathered, findings by dimension (fairness/transparency/accountability/safety/privacy), gap severity classification, remediation recommendations with owners and timelines, overall audit verdict, next audit schedule.
