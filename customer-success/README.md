# Customer Success & Feedback Systems

This section contains skills for customer success and feedback system work.

The goal is to help an agent support teams in running voice of customer programs, managing feedback loops, analyzing churn, and monitoring adoption health.

---

## What's inside

This domain covers the key jobs in customer success and feedback:

```text
voice of customer programs
    ↓
feedback intake & triage
    ↓
churn analysis & retention interventions
    ↓
adoption health monitoring
```

---

## Skills in this domain

1. **run-voc-program**
   Design and operate a Voice of Customer program that surfaces systematic insights from customers.

2. **triage-feedback-loop**
   Establish a feedback intake and triage system that routes insights to the right product teams.

3. **analyze-churn-retention**
   Analyze churn patterns, identify root causes, and design retention intervention strategies.

4. **monitor-adoption-health**
   Define and monitor adoption health metrics to identify at-risk accounts and expansion opportunities.
