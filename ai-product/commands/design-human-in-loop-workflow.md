---
description: Design workflows that appropriately balance AI automation with human oversight and intervention. Use this skill when a team is building an AI feature and needs to decide when and how humans stay in the loop.
---

Ask the user for: AI feature or workflow being designed, stakes level (low / medium / high consequences of errors), user segment interacting with the AI. Optionally: model capability assessment, known failure modes, regulatory or compliance context, user trust level with AI.

Apply the skill at skills/design-human-in-loop-workflow/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: workflow map with AI involvement per step, stakes assessment per step, intervention mode recommendation per step, review and override and escalation design, abstention and flagging criteria, feedback loop design, edge case handling.
