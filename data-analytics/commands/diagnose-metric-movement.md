description: Diagnose the root cause of an unexpected metric movement by systematically ruling out alternative explanations. Use this skill when a significant metric change has been detected and the team needs to understand why it happened.
---

Ask the user for: metric that changed (name and magnitude), time period of the change, recent product changes or events that could be relevant. Optionally: segment breakdowns, instrumentation audit results, external events, traffic source or acquisition channel breakdown.

Apply the skill at skills/diagnose-metric-movement/SKILL.md and REFERENCE.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: metric change summary, data quality check results, instrumentation issues found, timeline of product changes in the window, external confound assessment, segment breakdown analysis, ranked candidate causes, most probable cause with evidence, recommended next step to confirm.
