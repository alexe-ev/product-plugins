## Input
B2C subscription app, monthly plan.
Average revenue per user per month: $12.99
Gross margin: 72%
Monthly churn rate: 4.3%
CAC (blended): $38
CAC breakdown: Meta Ads $52, organic/referral $14

## Expected output
LTV calculation:
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate
LTV = ($12.99 × 0.72) / 0.043
LTV = $9.35 / 0.043
LTV = approximately $217

CAC:
Blended CAC: $38
By channel: Meta Ads $52, organic/referral $14

LTV:CAC ratio:
Blended: $217 / $38 = 5.7x
Meta Ads: $217 / $52 = 4.2x
Organic/referral: $217 / $14 = 15.5x

Payback period (blended):
Payback = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)
Payback = $38 / ($12.99 × 0.72) = $38 / $9.35 = approximately 4.1 months

Benchmark comparison:
5.7x blended LTV:CAC is healthy (benchmark: >3x is generally considered sustainable).
4.1-month payback is strong (benchmark: <12 months is considered healthy for B2C subscription).
Meta Ads at 4.2x is acceptable but tight — any churn increase or CAC rise would push it toward marginal.
Organic/referral at 15.5x is excellent; scaling this channel would materially improve blended economics.

Ratio interpretation:
Healthy overall. No immediate concern. Meta Ads channel warrants monitoring — the margin between acceptable and marginal is thin. A 1-point churn increase (4.3% → 5.3%) would drop blended LTV to ~$176 and blended ratio to 4.6x (still healthy, but Meta Ads would drop to 3.4x, borderline).

CAC by channel:
Meta $52 / Organic $14. The 3.7x CAC difference between channels makes the channel mix a significant driver of economic health. Increasing the organic share from current levels would reduce blended CAC without affecting LTV.

Top improvement levers with estimated impact:
1. Reduce monthly churn from 4.3% to 3.5%: LTV increases from $217 to $265 (+22%). Blended ratio improves from 5.7x to 7.0x. This is the highest-leverage single lever.
2. Grow organic/referral as share of new users: if organic grows from current share to 50% of new users, blended CAC drops significantly. Depends on current channel mix (not provided).
3. Reduce Meta CAC from $52 to $42 (through creative optimization or audience refinement): Meta channel ratio improves from 4.2x to 5.2x, moving it into the healthy zone with more margin.

Model at different scale:
If monthly churn rises to 6%: LTV drops to $156, blended ratio falls to 4.1x, Meta channel falls to 3.0x — marginal.
If churn improves to 3%: LTV rises to $311, blended ratio rises to 8.2x — strong economics, would support higher CAC tolerance.

Inputs used:
- ARPU: $12.99/month
- Gross margin: 72%

- Blended CAC: $38

Assumptions:
- LTV formula assumes constant monthly churn (geometric decay model)
- No expansion revenue included (single plan, no upsell)
- Gross margin is stable across cohorts
