---
description: Structure the trade-off decision between addressing technical debt and delivering new product value. Use this skill when a team needs to make explicit, principled decisions about when and how to invest in technical debt reduction.
---

Ask the user for: technical debt area or initiative, estimated time required to address, estimated impact of debt on velocity/reliability/user experience/risk. Optionally: engineering assessment of debt severity, user or business impact data, opportunity cost (what feature work is blocked or slowed).

Apply the skill at skills/manage-technical-debt-tradeoffs/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: debt characterization, current cost of debt, investment estimate, payback analysis, risk of deferral, recommendation (address now / schedule next cycle / accept as-is), stakeholder communication framing.
