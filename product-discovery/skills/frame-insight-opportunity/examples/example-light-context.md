## Input

Product: Finly — a B2C mobile app for personal finance.

Context: "From our research, we know users want to feel in control. We think there's an opportunity around goal-setting, but we haven't formally validated it."

## Expected output

Opportunity 1 (provisional):
- HMW statement: How might we help new users feel in control of their finances more quickly after sign-up, so that they have a reason to return to the app?
- Source insight: Users want to feel in control — the specific mechanism or feature that creates that feeling is not yet confirmed.
- Affected segment: Provisional — likely new users, but segment not confirmed from research.
- Potential impact: Medium (assumed) — directional potential is plausible but magnitude is unknown.
- Evidence quality: Assumed — "wanting control" is a broad and unspecific insight. What produces or signals control to users is not yet established.
- Risks: This opportunity statement is too broad to drive a clear build or test decision. Investing in any solution here risks solving the wrong version of the problem.
- Suggested next step: Narrow the insight first. Run additional research or re-analyse existing interviews to understand what "in control" means behaviourally — what would a user do or see that would give them that feeling?

Opportunity 2 (provisional):
- HMW statement: How might we make savings goal-setting feel valuable and easy in the early product experience, so that new users establish a forward-looking frame from the start?
- Source insight: Team believes goal-setting is connected to retention — not yet validated in research.
- Affected segment: New users (assumed).
- Potential impact: Unknown — depends on whether goal-setting actually drives retention, which has not been confirmed.
- Evidence quality: Assumed — based on team intuition, not user data.
- Risks: Investing in goal-setting improvements before validating the retention connection risks building the wrong thing.
- Suggested next step: Validate whether savings goal-setting correlates with Day 14 retention in product data before treating this as a prioritised opportunity.

Note: Both opportunities are provisional and not ready for roadmap prioritisation or stakeholder presentation without additional research or data validation.
