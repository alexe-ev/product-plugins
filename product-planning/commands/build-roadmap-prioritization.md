---
description: Build and prioritize a feature or initiative roadmap using structured frameworks. Use this skill when a team has a backlog or list of initiatives and needs a coherent, prioritized plan.
---

Ask the user for: list of features, initiatives, or requests (even rough), goals or OKRs this roadmap should serve. Optionally: prioritization criteria or weights, team capacity or constraints, customer segment priorities, technical dependencies.

Apply the skill at skills/build-roadmap-prioritization/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: prioritized initiative list with rationale, roadmap structure (now / next / later or time-horizon based), goal mapping for each initiative, trade-off decisions (what was deprioritized), dependencies and sequencing notes, assumptions.
