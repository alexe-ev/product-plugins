---
description: Write a clear, concise product strategy document that aligns teams on direction, priorities, and trade-offs. Use this skill when a team needs a written strategy artifact that can serve as a decision-making anchor.
---

Ask the user for: product area or scope, strategic direction/goals/key decisions to document. Optionally: existing strategy artifacts, OKRs or portfolio decisions, competitive context, product-market fit assessment.

Apply the skill at skills/write-product-strategy-doc/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: context section, strategic approach and key choices, explicit trade-offs and what's out of scope, success definition (12-month horizon), key risks and mitigations, open decisions requiring alignment.
