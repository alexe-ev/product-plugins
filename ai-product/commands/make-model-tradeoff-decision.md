---
description: Structure the trade-off decision between AI models, providers, or approaches for a given product use case. Use this skill when a team needs to choose between AI options with different capability, cost, latency, and risk profiles.
---

Ask the user for: product use case requiring AI, candidate models or approaches to compare (at least 2), key requirements (capability needs, latency budget, cost constraints). Optionally: quality evaluation data from initial testing, user tolerance for latency and errors, regulatory or data privacy constraints, build vs. buy vs. fine-tune context.

Apply the skill at skills/make-model-tradeoff-decision/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: use case requirements, candidate models/approaches, evaluation matrix, key trade-offs identified, recommendation with rationale, what the chosen option sacrifices, re-evaluation triggers.
