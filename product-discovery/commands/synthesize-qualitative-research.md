description: Synthesize user interviews, JTBD analysis, and qualitative data into actionable product insights. Use this skill when a team has raw qualitative data and needs structured insights.
---

Ask the user for: raw qualitative data (interview notes, quotes, feedback, JTBD statements). Optionally: research questions the team was trying to answer, user segment context, hypotheses being tested.

Apply the skill at skills/synthesize-qualitative-research/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: research questions (stated or inferred), key themes with supporting evidence, top insights (3-5, actionable), JTBD summary (if applicable), outliers and edge cases, research gaps, confidence level.
