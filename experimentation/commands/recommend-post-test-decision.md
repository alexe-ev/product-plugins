description: Recommend what to do after an experiment based on the result quality, effect size, and business context. Use this skill when the user has a completed experiment and needs a practical product decision such as roll out, rerun, iterate, segment-rollout, or reject.

Ask the user for: experiment result summary, quality or validity assessment. Optionally: business context, engineering cost, strategic priority, rollout risk, segment-specific effects, guardrail performance.

Apply the skill at skills/recommend-post-test-decision/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: recommended action, rationale, risks, what should happen next, missing business inputs that limit recommendation strength.
