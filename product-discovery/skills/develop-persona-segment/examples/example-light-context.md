## Input

Product: Finly — a B2C mobile app for personal finance.

Context: "We think we have two types of users: those who care a lot about savings and those who just want to check their transactions. We haven't done formal research to confirm this."

## Expected output

Persona 1 (provisional — assumption-based): The Savings-Focused User

Name and archetype: To be confirmed in research

Primary goals and JTBD (assumed):
- Goal: Build savings toward a meaningful goal; feel financially responsible and in control
- JTBD (hypothesised): "When I want to make progress on saving, I want to see how far I've come and what's left, so I feel motivated to keep going."

Key pain points (assumed): Suspected friction around progress visibility — if savings progress isn't shown clearly, motivation may drop. Not confirmed.

Behavioural patterns: Assumed to engage more frequently and with savings features specifically — not yet confirmed from data.

Evidence quality: Assumed — based on team intuition. Not validated.

Research gaps: What specifically motivates goal-setting behaviour? What does "savings-focused" mean behaviourally vs. attitudinally? Does this persona exist as a distinct type or is it a stage that all users move through?

---

Persona 2 (provisional — assumption-based): The Transaction Checker

Name and archetype: To be confirmed in research

Primary goals and JTBD (assumed):
- Goal: Know where money went; avoid financial surprises
- JTBD (hypothesised): "When I'm not sure where my money went, I want a quick way to check, so I can feel reassured or decide to adjust."

Key pain points (assumed): Effort to review transactions; lack of clarity in spend categories. Not confirmed.

Behavioural patterns: Assumed to engage reactively and infrequently — not yet confirmed.

Evidence quality: Assumed — based on team intuition. Not validated.

Research gaps: Is this a truly distinct segment or is it what all users look like before they discover savings features? Would a simpler product mode serve this user better?

---

Note: These are hypothesis personas only. Do not use for product, design, or messaging decisions until validated with research. Recommended next step: run 4–6 user interviews with users matching each suspected type to validate motivations, pain points, and context of use before treating these as decision-ready segments.
