---
description: Design and refine team rituals and operating rhythms that build alignment and operating discipline. Use this skill when a team wants to improve how they work together through intentional recurring practices.
---

Ask the user for: team type and composition, current state of team rituals (even if "none"), key team goals or challenges. Optionally: team size, remote vs. in-person vs. hybrid setup, current pain points in team dynamics, desired culture or working norms.

Apply the skill at skills/design-team-rituals/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: team needs assessment, current ritual audit, recommended ritual set with cadence/duration/format/facilitation/purpose, ritual ownership and maintenance plan, ritual effectiveness review process, what to stop/start/continue.
