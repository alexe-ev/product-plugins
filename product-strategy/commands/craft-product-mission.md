---
description: Write a clear, actionable product mission statement that defines what the product does, for whom, and why it exists today. Use this skill when a team needs to separate mission (today) from vision (future) and make the mission specific enough to guide decisions.
---

Ask the user for: target user or customer segment, core value the product delivers, product area or type. Optionally: existing mission or vision drafts, business model context, OKRs or strategic goals.

Apply the skill at skills/craft-product-mission/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: mission statement, who it serves, core job or problem addressed, value delivered, specificity test (what would this mission say no to?), confidence level (provisional / context-informed / validated), open questions.
