---
description: Establish or refresh the operating norms and working agreements that govern how a product team works together. Use this skill when a team lacks explicit norms or existing norms are no longer working.
---

Ask the user for: team type, size, and structure, known friction points or gaps in current working norms. Optionally: existing team agreements or culture documents, team values or principles, input from recent retros or feedback, remote/hybrid/in-person context.

Apply the skill at skills/build-operating-norms/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: norm domains covered, explicit norm statements per domain, how norms were co-created (or should be), norm review cadence, accountability approach for norm violations, version and date.
