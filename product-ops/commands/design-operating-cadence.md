---
description: Design or improve the operating rhythm for a product team including meetings, reviews, reporting, and planning cycles. Use this skill when a team's operating process is ad hoc, broken, or needs to be established.
---

Ask the user for: team structure (product, engineering, design, leadership involved), current state of the operating process. Optionally: team size, current pain points, planning horizon (quarterly, 6-week cycles, etc.), stakeholder reporting requirements.

Apply the skill at skills/design-operating-cadence/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: meeting cadence design (recurring meetings with frequency, duration, goal, attendees), planning cycle design, review and reporting schedule, decision-making framework (who decides what), async communication norms, known failure modes and mitigations.
