description: Apply structured ethical frameworks to evaluate product decisions and identify potential harms. Use this skill when a product decision raises ethical concerns or involves trade-offs between user wellbeing and business goals.
---

Ask the user for: product decision or feature being evaluated, user groups affected. Optionally: business goal being served, known concerns or objections, regulatory context, relevant demographic or equity considerations.

Apply the skill at skills/apply-ethical-decision-framework/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: affected user group map, potential harm identification by type and severity, ethical evaluation across frameworks, benefit and burden distribution, recommended adjustments to reduce harm, escalation triggers.
