---
description: Audit the current state of product processes to identify bottlenecks, inconsistencies, and improvement opportunities. Use this skill when a product team wants to understand where their operating model is breaking down.
---

Ask the user for: team type and size, processes to audit. Optionally: team pain points or frustrations, recent failures or near-misses with process as a factor, existing process documentation, cross-functional inputs on friction with the product team.

Apply the skill at skills/audit-product-process/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: process inventory, assessment per process (existence / consistency / effectiveness), pain points by process, issue classification, cross-functional friction points, priority improvement list with rationale, quick wins vs. structural changes.
