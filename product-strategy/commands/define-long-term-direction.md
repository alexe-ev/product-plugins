---
description: Translate vision and mission into a clear long-term product direction — the strategic choices, priorities, and trajectory that guide where the product will go over the next 2–4 years. Use this skill when a team has a vision and mission but lacks a coherent strategic direction connecting them to current decisions.
---

Ask the user for: product vision or aspirational destination, product mission or current operational focus, business goals for the planning horizon. Optionally: current product capabilities and gaps, key market or user trends, competitive landscape, bets or investment areas under consideration.

Apply the skill at skills/define-long-term-direction/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: strategic direction narrative (2-5 sentences), planning horizon, trajectory map (grow / maintain / deprioritize areas), defining strategic choices (with rationale), success picture at end of horizon, key assumptions, open questions or decision points ahead.
