---
description: Plan and execute a stakeholder alignment process for a product decision or initiative. Use this skill when a team needs to build genuine alignment — not just sign-off — across multiple stakeholders.
---

Ask the user for: decision or initiative requiring alignment, stakeholders involved and their current stances. Optionally: stakeholder map, prior alignment attempts and what went wrong, decision timeline, key concerns or objections known in advance.

Apply the skill at skills/run-stakeholder-alignment/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: alignment goal, must-align stakeholder list with stances, engagement sequence, preparation per key stakeholder, success definition, follow-up plan, contingency if alignment isn't reached.
