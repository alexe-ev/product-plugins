description: Analyze and recommend pricing structures and packaging options aligned with value delivery and business goals. Use this skill when a team needs to make or review pricing and packaging decisions.
---

Ask the user for: product or feature being priced, target customer segment, business model (subscription, usage-based, one-time, freemium). Optionally: current pricing structure, customer feedback on pricing, competitor pricing, unit economics data, value metrics.

Apply the skill at skills/support-pricing-packaging/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: value metric identification, pricing model options with trade-offs, recommended packaging structure and tiers, rationale per tier, validation steps before finalizing, risks and open questions.
