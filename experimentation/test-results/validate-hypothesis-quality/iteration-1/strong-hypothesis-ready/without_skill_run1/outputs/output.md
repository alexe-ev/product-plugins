# Hypothesis Validation Report
## Video Reviews vs Text Reviews for Mobile Electronics Category

**Hypothesis:** Replacing text reviews with video reviews on product cards for mobile users in the Electronics category will increase add-to-cart conversion by at least 3% because video enhances product trust and reduces purchase uncertainty.

---

## Executive Summary
**Validation Status:** ✅ **STRONG HYPOTHESIS - READY FOR EXPERIMENTATION**

This hypothesis demonstrates solid foundational thinking with clear directional expectations and reasonable assumptions. The mechanism is well-articulated and the 3% lift target is achievable.

---

## Strengths of This Hypothesis

### 1. **Clear Causal Mechanism**
- The hypothesis articulates WHY the change should work: video builds trust and reduces uncertainty
- Trust and uncertainty reduction are evidence-backed drivers of purchase conversion
- The pathway is logical and testable

### 2. **Specific Target Segment**
- Defines target clearly: Mobile users + Electronics category
- This segmentation is strategic (electronics buyers often need reassurance about product quality/durability)
- Mobile constraint justifies focusing on high-impact formats (video is more scannable than long text reviews)

### 3. **Realistic Lift Expectation**
- 3% minimum lift is ambitious but achievable for e-commerce
- Not overly optimistic (avoiding 10%+ expectations that often disappoint)
- Demonstrates understanding that incremental improvements compound

### 4. **Evidence-Based Reasoning**
- Video content generally increases trust and engagement (supported by broader e-commerce research)
- User-generated video reviews reduce information asymmetry
- Electronics category is amenable to video (allows demonstration of features, build quality, unboxing)

---

## Areas to Strengthen Before Experiment

### 1. **Clarify the Control Condition**
- **Current state:** What exactly is the baseline? Text reviews, star ratings, or both?
- **Recommendation:** Define whether video supplements or replaces text, or if it's a mixed approach

### 2. **Video Quality Standards**
- **Risk:** Low-quality, hard-to-watch videos could backfire and decrease trust
- **Strengthen by:** Specify video criteria (resolution, length, lighting standards, required content elements)
- **Question:** Will you curate videos or show all user videos as-is?

### 3. **Mobile Implementation Details**
- **Consideration:** How will videos auto-play on mobile? (Data usage concern)
- **Recommendation:** Specify autoplay policy (muted, on-WiFi only, play-on-click, etc.)
- **Impact:** Implementation affects user friction and true causal effect

### 4. **Segment Definition Nuance**
- Electronics is broad (appliances, computers, accessories, smart home)
- **Recommendation:** Consider whether effect size differs by subcategory
- **Hypothesis could improve by:** Stating if you expect uniform 3% lift across all electronics or targeting high-uncertainty categories (e.g., cameras, laptops)

### 5. **Baseline Conversion Rate**
- **Missing:** Unclear what the current add-to-cart conversion is
- **Why it matters:** 3% lift on 2% baseline conversion = 6% relative lift; 3% lift on 30% baseline = 10% relative lift
- **Strengthen by:** Stating current mobile add-to-cart rate for electronics

### 6. **Confounding Variables to Control**
- **Consider accounting for:**
  - Seasonal demand variations
  - Price points (expensive items may show different lift patterns)
  - Existing review count (products with few reviews may benefit more)
  - Product age/newness

---

## Experiment Design Considerations

### Sample Size & Duration
- **Assumption:** Electronics category has sufficient daily mobile traffic to detect 3% lift
- **Validate:** Confirm statistical power (likely need 2-4 weeks depending on traffic)

### Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Add-to-cart conversion rate (✓ clearly stated)
- **Secondary metrics** to track:
  - Video watch rate and watch-through percentage
  - Review credibility perception (if surveyed)
  - Video impression time per product page
  - Add-to-cart value (check for order value changes)
  - Potential cannibalization: Does video engagement reduce text review engagement?

### Risk Mitigation
1. **Performance:** Ensure video loading doesn't slow mobile page speed
2. **Fallback:** Have plan if video availability is low (some products lack user videos)
3. **Quality:** Monitor for negative reviews or low-quality videos harming trust
