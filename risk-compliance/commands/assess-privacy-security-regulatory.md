Apply the skill at skills/assess-privacy-security-regulatory/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: data types and classification, applicable regulations, privacy risk assessment, security risk assessment, consent and transparency requirements, user rights implications, items requiring legal or security review, recommended mitigations.
