---
description: Define non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability, accessibility, compliance) for a product initiative. Use this skill when product requirements are functional but non-functional constraints haven't been specified.
---

Ask the user for: product feature or initiative, expected scale and usage patterns, user segment and context. Optionally: regulatory context, performance baselines or benchmarks, security requirements from legal or compliance, accessibility standards in use.

Apply the skill at skills/define-non-functional-requirements/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: NFR inventory by category, each NFR with description/acceptance criteria/hard requirement vs. target, cost or timeline implications, validation approach per NFR, NFRs requiring third-party assessment, open questions for engineering or compliance.
