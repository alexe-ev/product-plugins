Ask the user for: product or feature being launched, target launch timeline, target audience. Optionally: readiness criteria list, stakeholder list, channel options, success metrics.

Apply the skill at skills/plan-product-launch/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: launch readiness criteria by area, launch audience and rollout strategy, channel plan and messaging brief, pre-launch / launch day / post-launch checklist, success metrics and measurement plan, rollback criteria and process, stakeholder communication plan.
