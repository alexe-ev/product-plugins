---
description: Build and compare forecasting scenarios to support product and business planning decisions. Use this skill when a team needs to project outcomes under different assumptions.
---

Ask the user for: metric to forecast (revenue, users, CAC, churn, etc.), current baseline and growth context, time horizon. Optionally: key assumptions to vary, strategic initiatives that affect the forecast, historical trends, competitive or market context.

Apply the skill at skills/run-forecasting-scenarios/SKILL.md and REFERENCE.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: metric and time horizon, baseline, key assumptions per scenario, scenario table (base / upside / downside with projections), sensitivity analysis, planning recommendation, confidence level.
