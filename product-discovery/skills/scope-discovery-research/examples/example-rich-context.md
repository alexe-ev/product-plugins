## Input

Product: Finly — a B2C mobile app for personal finance.

Context: The team has identified that new users disengage after week 1. Retention data confirms a drop from 34% to 22% at Day 14. The team needs to decide between two possible investment directions: (A) build a progress visibility feature to show savings momentum, or (B) redesign the notification system to create re-entry triggers. Research must produce a clear recommendation. Timeline: 3 weeks. Resources: 1 researcher, access to user base for recruiting.

## Expected output

Research goal: Understand why new Finly users disengage after their first week, and determine whether a progress visibility feature or a notification redesign is the higher-impact investment to improve Day 14 retention.

Key research questions (prioritised):
1. What do new users expect to happen in the first two weeks after sign-up — and does the current experience match that expectation?
2. What triggers a return visit after day 1, and what prevents it?
3. What signals of progress, if any, feel meaningful or motivating to users?
4. What role do notifications currently play — do they prompt return visits or are they ignored?
5. What are users doing instead of returning to Finly when they disengage?

Known vs. unknown:
- Known: Day 14 retention dropped; support tickets reference "nothing changed after week 1"; two plausible product directions exist
- Unknown: Root cause of disengagement; whether churn is driven by expectation mismatch, motivational gap, or poor re-entry triggers; whether this pattern differs by user type

Methods per question:
- Q1–Q3: Semi-structured user interviews (30–45 min) — best method for exploring expectations, motivations, and emotional context of engagement
- Q4: In-app micro-survey or diary study — captures real-time reaction to notifications rather than retrospective recall
- Q5: Closing question within interviews — indirect inquiry to surface competing alternatives and avoidance patterns

Participant criteria:
- New Finly users who signed up in the last 90 days
- Mix: 4–5 churned users (no session after day 7) and 4–5 retained users (3+ sessions in first two weeks)
- All participants: connected at least one bank account
- Recruiting via email to recent sign-up cohort; offer small incentive for churned users to improve response rate

Timeline:
- Week 1: Recruit participants, conduct 8–10 interviews
- Week 2: Synthesise findings using affinity mapping; run micro-survey or diary component
- Week 3: Produce findings document and decision recommendation; share with product team

Output format: Written research summary with top insights, a clear recommendation on which investment direction the evidence supports, and open questions for a potential follow-up.

Risk and contingency plan: If churned users are difficult to reach via email, supplement with analysis of support tickets and exit survey data from the same cohort. If fewer than 4 churned users participate, treat findings as directional only and flag the limitation.
