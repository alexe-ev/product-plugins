# Customer Success & Feedback Systems

Skills for running voice-of-customer programs, triaging feedback, analyzing churn, monitoring adoption health, and building retention playbooks.

---

## Skills in this domain

**Voice of customer**
1. `run-voc-program` — Design and run a structured voice-of-customer program
2. `design-customer-feedback-survey` — Design surveys that generate reliable, actionable feedback data

**Feedback intake & synthesis**
3. `triage-feedback-loop` — Design feedback intake and triage workflows
4. `synthesize-feedback-themes` — Turn high-volume feedback into structured themes and product signals

**Churn & retention**
5. `analyze-churn-retention` — Analyze churn patterns and identify root causes
6. `design-retention-playbook` — Design CS retention plays triggered by churn signals

**Adoption health**
7. `monitor-adoption-health` — Define and monitor adoption health metrics
8. `build-customer-health-score` — Build a predictive customer health score model
