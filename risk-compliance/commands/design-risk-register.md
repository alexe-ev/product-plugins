---
description: Design and populate a risk register for a product initiative or program. Use this skill when risks need to be tracked, owned, and actively managed over the lifetime of an initiative.
---

Ask the user for: initiative or product area, known risks (or a recent pre-mortem or risk assessment). Optionally: risk tolerance framing from leadership, governance or compliance requirements, cross-team or vendor dependencies, historical risks from similar initiatives.

Apply the skill at skills/design-risk-register/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: risk register scope and taxonomy, risk entries with likelihood/impact/severity/trigger/owner/mitigation/contingency, update and review cadence, escalation criteria and path, archive criteria, risk register owner.
