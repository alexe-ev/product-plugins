---
description: Design a customer feedback survey that generates reliable, actionable data. Use this skill when a team needs to collect structured feedback at scale without the bias and noise that comes from poorly designed surveys.
---

Ask the user for: survey goal (what decision will this survey inform?), target respondents. Optionally: prior survey designs or results, feedback frequency requirements, tooling constraints (Typeform, Google Forms, in-app survey tool), regulatory constraints on data collection.

Apply the skill at skills/design-customer-feedback-survey/SKILL.md.

If required inputs are missing, list them and stop. Output: primary survey question, survey type and rationale, question set (5 or fewer), question quality review (bias check), distribution plan and trigger logic, expected response rate and sample size, analysis plan.
